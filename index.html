PROJECT 01

Cattle & Dairy Business Digitalisation

Development of an integrated Excel-based operational and accounting management system supporting the transition from paper-based administration to structured digital records.

  • Financial transaction records and chart of accounts
  • General ledger and financial balances
  • Accounts receivable and payable
  • Currency-related calculations and reporting
  • Business-specific operational information

PROJECT 02

Construction Project Financial Control

A structured Excel-based workbook developed for monitoring construction project finances, cost movements and profitability.

  • Project configuration and contract tracking
  • Estimated versus actual expenses
  • Invoicing and payments
  • Cash position and collections
  • Approved change orders
  • Financial and profitability summaries
Solution scope

The workbook brings related financial information into a structured project-control environment. Demonstrated functionality is distinguished from any implementation outcomes not yet verified.

Excel-based reporting and operational tools supporting production and distribution activities across regional sales operations.

  • Consolidated management information
Responsibilities and approach

Developed and standardised reporting structures across regional activities, supported operational users and prepared consolidated information for business management.
