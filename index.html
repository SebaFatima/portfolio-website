PROJECT 01Cattle & Dairy Business DigitalisationIntegrated operational and financial management system for a cattle and dairy business. Excel system previewUpload dairy/preview.png ⌄

Project overview. From paper-based administration and disconnected records to a structured Excel system for financial control, account-level tracking and recurring reporting.

The Business Challenge

The business relied heavily on paper records and separate administrative processes to manage financial and operational information. Transactions, obligations, payments, accounts and livestock-and-dairy information needed a usable digital structure for employees with varying computer experience.

My Role & Approach

I investigated how information moved through the business, what each person recorded and which reports were needed. Working closely with the accountant, I translated the accounting requirements into workbook architecture, calculations and reports. The accountant supplied accounting rules and domain expertise; I designed, developed and supported the technical solution, guided users and refined it based on feedback.

The Solution

An integrated Excel-based operational and financial management workbook, connecting structured transaction records, a chart of accounts, reusable calculations and reporting views. Users can move from account-level transaction detail to management-level summaries in the same system.

Operational Value & Outcomes

The system supported the transition from paper administration to digital financial and operational workflows. It has remained in active use for more than six months, with continued support and improvements. Users reported that administrative work became faster and more manageable; no unverified percentage improvement is claimed.

Technical Features

  • Transaction records and chart of accounts
  • General ledger with selected account movements, debit/credit detail and balances
  • Receivables and payables: summaries and responsible-person details
  • Trial balance, income statement and balance-sheet views
  • Local-currency and US-dollar reporting, exchange-rate calculations and variance tracking
  • Defined categories, input controls, report filters and user instructions
  • Business-specific operational records

How users work with it

Users record and classify transactions, select an account or responsible person, and access relevant detailed movements or financial summaries for review.

PROJECT 02Construction Project Financial ControlProject budgets, actual costs, invoicing, cash collections and profitability reporting in one workbook. Excel system previewUpload construction/preview.png ⌄

Project overview. A project-centred Excel solution bringing financial inputs and management summaries together without repeated manual calculations across separate sections.

The Business Challenge

Construction projects involve moving financial elements: contract values, planned spending, actual costs, invoices, collections and approved changes. Disconnected records make budget performance, billing position and profitability difficult to track consistently.

My Role & Approach

I designed and developed a customised workbook organised around project setup, monitoring and reporting. I separated data-entry areas from calculated summaries and provided user instructions identifying the sections requiring manual input.

The Solution

The system links project setup, expense transactions, invoicing and approved contract changes within one financial-control workbook. Its summaries calculate estimated versus actual spending, billing progress, cash collected versus outstanding amounts, and project profitability.

Operational Value & Outcomes

The deliverable is a completed custom project financial-control workbook connecting financial records and reporting functions. Final client adoption has not been established, so this case study describes developed functionality rather than claiming confirmed savings or implementation results.

Technical Features

  • Project and contract configuration
  • Budget allocation by cost category
  • Expense and payment transaction ledger
  • Budget-versus-actual amounts and variance percentages
  • Invoice register and billing progress
  • Approved change-order register
  • Contract, cash-position and profitability summaries
  • Designated input areas and automatically updated outputs

How users work with it

Users set up a project and enter expenses, invoices and approved changes in designated tables. Related project summaries then reflect those records.

PROJECT 03Poultry Sales & Distribution ReportingStandardised Excel reporting for regional sales, inventory movements, collections and management information. Excel system previewUpload poultry/preview.png ⌄

Project overview. Structured operational reporting across four regional sales areas, linking sales activity, product movements and outstanding balances.

The Business Challenge

The poultry production and egg distribution company operated across four regional sales areas. Different recording formats made consolidation difficult for management and accounting. Sales, inventory, collections, returns and expenses needed consistent reporting despite unreliable connectivity and file-conflict issues.

My Role & Approach

I built the regional sales workbooks from the ground up, consulted managers about requirements and how staff would use the files, standardised inputs and calculations, and prepared instructions. I supported adoption and troubleshooting, while regional managers trained their teams. I continued adapting the files as workflows changed.

The Solution

The Excel reporting process standardised regional records covering product quantities, inventory movement, pricing, sales, collections, pending balances, returns, losses, donations and expenses. Information was consolidated into central management and accounting views through a defined transfer process suited to connectivity limitations.

Operational Value & Outcomes

The regional workbooks were adopted and continued in use. They established more consistent recording and consolidation for management reporting. This work was part of broader operational improvements, including other inventory, production and administrative reporting responsibilities.

Technical Features

  • Regional records with dates, invoice references and customer or area information
  • Quantities, packaging/unit conversions, prices and sales amounts
  • Inventory received, distributed, sold and remaining
  • Returns, donations, losses and expenses
  • Collections and outstanding customer balances
  • Monthly sales, quantity, price and collection summaries
  • Standardised input fields, categories, filters and controls
  • Defined transfer and consolidation process across regions

Related responsibilities

Additional work in the company included warehouse and feed-inventory controls, production reporting, recurring accounting-spreadsheet tasks, HR workbook improvements, training and documentation. These are related experience, not features attributed to this sales workbook.
